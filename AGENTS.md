# Principle

**[Set goals and design from what you know now.](skills/principle-code-simplicity/SKILL.md)** Ground any goal in the current situation, confirmed needs, and known constraints. Use unconfirmed assumptions to identify what to verify. Revisit the goal and approach as new evidence becomes available.
